# Eval Prompt: AI Art Style

```text
I want to generate many item icons with AI for my fantasy game. Batches are fast, but the icons start looking like they belong to different games. Propose a safe small pipeline before I generate hundreds of them.
```

## Good Output Should

- Identify the tradeoff between content volume and style consistency.
- Require one approved canonical example before batch generation.
- Add style rules, review gates, and normalization steps.
- Avoid treating generation as the whole pipeline.
- Include in-game scale or contact-sheet verification.
