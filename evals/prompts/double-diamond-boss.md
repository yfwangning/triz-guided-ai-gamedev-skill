# Eval Prompt: Double-Diamond Boss

```text
Boss phase 2 is boring, but do not make it cheap. Use double-diamond mode. Do not converge too early; explore possible causes and more creative options first.
```

## Good Output Should

- Use a double-diamond structure.
- Diverge on multiple possible meanings of "boring".
- Converge on one first tradeoff to solve.
- Diverge on varied solution directions, including safe, weird, systemic, content-light, and ambitious options.
- Converge on a smallest playable test.
- Include what would prove the test works and what would prove it failed.
- Avoid making every option a small variation of the same attack.
